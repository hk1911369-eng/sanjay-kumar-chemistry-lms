## 4. Important distinction: author/title records are not interchangeable

The Akkharpatra Chemistry records above name Mahbub Hasan Linkon, Dr. Md. Abdul Karim and Dr. Md. Nurul Islam. Other HSC Chemistry books found in the previous tranche include works by Haradhan Nag and Dr. Saroj Kanti Singh Hazari, Sanjit Kumar Guha, and an Edulite editorial board. These are separate book families, not author aliases or one combined text.

Likewise, Akkharpatra Biology titles have multiple author/editor combinations and must not be merged with the separate Gazi Publishers books by Gazi Azmol or Hassan Book House books by Dr. Mohammad Abul Hasan.
