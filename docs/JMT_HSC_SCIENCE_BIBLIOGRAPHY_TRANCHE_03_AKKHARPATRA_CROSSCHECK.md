## 6. New curriculum and catalogue insight

The publisher catalogue shows a broad commercial HSC subject portfolio extending well beyond science. It can help JMT discover titles in humanities and business studies, but it is not an official national booklist. A publisher's claim that a book is NCTB-approved or aligned to a curriculum is a claim to verify against the official NCTB source and the applicable cohort syllabus.

A new category is also visible: **Mathematical Physics** and **Mathematical Chemistry** paper-specific books. They should be classified separately from the standard Physics and Chemistry textbooks, and JMT should not assume they are required for every HSC learner.

## 7. Content-quality checks to apply after lawful access

- Physics: dimensional consistency, vector/scalar definitions, derivation assumptions, graph axes/units, significant figures, uncertainty treatment, calculator modes and worked-example arithmetic.
- Chemistry: balancing, oxidation states, equilibrium assumptions, thermochemical signs/units, organic nomenclature, reaction conditions, analytical chemistry, concentration calculations and significant figures.
- Biology: current nomenclature, taxonomic hierarchy, genetics notation, diagram accuracy/labels, ecological definitions, human physiology, practical specimen/slide descriptions and scientific-name spelling.
- Higher Mathematics: theorem hypotheses, domain/range, proof steps, calculus conditions, coordinate geometry, vector conventions, matrix operations, graph scales and answer verification.
- ICT: HTML conformance, C compiler assumptions, input/output examples, algorithm/flowchart consistency, SQL dialect/SQLite compatibility, database normalization, network/security terminology and software-version screenshots.
- Practical books: apparatus availability, safety, measurement procedure, observation tables, sample data provenance, uncertainty/error analysis, viva accuracy and alignment to actual board practical tasks.
