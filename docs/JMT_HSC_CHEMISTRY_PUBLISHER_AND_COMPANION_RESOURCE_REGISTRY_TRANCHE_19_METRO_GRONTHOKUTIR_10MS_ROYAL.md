## 5. Cross-publisher resource taxonomy

| Resource type | Examples in this tranche | JMT use |

| Core theory textbook | Metro Chemistry First/Second Paper; Grontho Kutir main books | Edition-specific syllabus mapping after contents review |

## 6. Data-integrity and content-review rules

1. Store the source-specific byline exactly as printed or catalogued; maintain a separate canonical person/entity record.
2. Keep cohort, publication year, edition and language separate.
3. Treat retailer product descriptions with contradictory names as unresolved, not as canonical author data.
4. Do not infer an ISBN from a SKU or retailer product ID.
5. Keep exercise books and master books separate from NCTB textbooks and publisher theory books.
6. Do not ingest full copyrighted text without explicit rights.
7. Check every mathematical solution, chemical equation, mechanism, formula, units and worked answer independently.
8. Validate practical instructions for chemical safety, reagent concentration, PPE, waste and viva.
9. Treat “common”, “sure marks”, “100% preparation” and historical-repeat percentages as marketing claims until independently tested.
