## 8. Data-integrity findings and schema implications

1. Keep Bangla and English editions distinct; do not merge by translated title alone.
2. Keep Exam 2027 and Exam 2028 records distinct, even when authors and title are unchanged.
3. Do not assume First Paper and Second Paper share ISBN, page count, language or edition date.
4. Store source-supplied identifiers with a type and validation status. The 22-digit PBS values for Lecture Chemistry 2026 are not ISBN-13 values; the Hasan Book House Physics First Paper string beginning 987 also needs validation.
5. Retain conflicting page counts as source observations until physical copies are inspected.
6. Store publisher, imprint, retailer, institution and source type separately. A bookseller is not the publisher.
7. Store the institution booklist's session and school scope; do not promote it to national status.
8. Keep textbook, parallel reference, solution book, practical manual, practice book and test paper as different resource types.

## 9. Content-review priorities

### Physics
- Independently recompute representative derivations and numerical solutions; verify dimensions, sign conventions, vector directions and units.
- Review electricity, magnetism, optics, thermodynamics and modern-physics examples against the verified cohort syllabus.
- Check that worked examples distinguish idealized assumptions from real experimental conditions.
- Compare translated terminology and equations across Bangla and English editions without assuming page-for-page equivalence.

### Chemistry
- Audit reaction conditions, equilibrium expressions, electrochemical conventions, organic mechanisms, nomenclature and stoichiometric calculations.
- Verify constants, significant figures, units, state symbols and exceptions.
- Check whether examples and exercises map to the actual cohort's learning outcomes and current board assessment blueprint.
