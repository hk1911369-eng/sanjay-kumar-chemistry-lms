## 8. Data-integrity and product-design findings

1. Statistics First/Second Paper and the practical notebook are separate resources.
2. Lecture's current 2028 Statistics First Paper has a visible 2026 edition record; its Second Paper is only a Rokomari product lead with incomplete metadata.
3. Panjeree's retrieved Statistics First Paper is Exam 2027, while its Second Paper discovery listing is Exam 2028. Do not assume the two are a matched same-cohort pair.
4. Grantha Kutir Statistics First Paper's title and retailer description contain author/approval claims, but ISBN is blank and the structured author field is missing.
5. Abacus Agriculture First Paper is Exam 2027; it cannot establish the current Exam 2028 Second Paper byline.
6. Lecture Agriculture Second Paper is a 2027 supplement, not a current core textbook.
7. Newton's Agriculture/Statistics products are described as practical resources and have 2023 editions; their title/author/extent fields need physical verification.
8. Long publisher identifiers must be validated before they are stored as ISBNs.
9. Store cohort, resource type, paper and language at edition level.
10. Do not treat commercial product claims of “NCTB approved” as official approval without the actual NCTB instrument.
