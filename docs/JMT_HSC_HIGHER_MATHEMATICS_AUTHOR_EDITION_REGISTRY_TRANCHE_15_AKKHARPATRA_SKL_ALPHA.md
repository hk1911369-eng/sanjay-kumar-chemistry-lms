## 4. Cross-publisher findings

1. The same publisher may release theory textbooks, complete solutions and practice/reference books. Their resource types must not be merged.
2. The same paper may have different edition numbers and page counts in Bengali and English.
3. Publisher catalogue and detail pages can conflict even within one publisher (Akkharpatra First Paper 776 vs 816 pages; Second Paper 736 vs 752).
4. Retailer author headlines can omit contributors listed on another product page. Preserve raw source-specific bylines.
5. SKL records show a contributor attribution difference: Rokomari lists both Ketab Uddin and Nazrul Islam; PBS product headlines often show Nazrul Islam alone.
6. Alpha's older records contain additional contributor names (A. F. M. Abdur Rahman; M. A. Jabbar) not established for every 2026 edition.
7. No product listing establishes NCTB prescription, content correctness or educational superiority.

## 5. Mathematics content QA requirements

For each sampled edition:
- Independently solve representative problems in algebra, matrices/determinants, vectors, straight lines, circles, trigonometry, functions, limits, differentiation, integration, probability and relevant geometry.
- Check every formula's conditions and domain restrictions.
- Verify sign conventions, units, rounding, notation and calculator assumptions.
- Check whether solution books reproduce the exact problem statements and edition numbering of their matching textbook.
- Test alternate solution methods and identify steps where a result is correct but reasoning is invalid.
- Validate examples against the target cohort's syllabus and learning outcomes; do not treat university admission enrichment as required HSC content.
- Review Bengali and English mathematical terminology for consistency.
