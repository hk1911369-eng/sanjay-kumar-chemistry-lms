## 5. Important cross-source corrections and integrity warnings

1. **Abacus ISBN collision:** PBS gives ISBN 9789843376725 for both Chemistry First Paper 2026 and Practical Chemistry reprint 2024. Store this as a conflict, not a confirmed ISBN for both titles.
2. **Abacus author-role ambiguity:** publisher catalogue, product description and retailer author fields do not consistently show the same full contributor statement. Record source-specific roles and await title-page evidence.
3. **Hasan Book House author history:** Haradhan Nag alone appears as the main entry in a 2012 library catalogue; a 2025 retailer lists Hazari and Nag together. Edition-specific authorship must be preserved.
4. **Systech cohort distinction:** its 2026 ICT textbook and 2023 question-answer book are separate resource types and cohorts.
5. **Kazi metadata quality:** current publisher-owned catalogue and title-page evidence are insufficient in this pass; secondary retailer data remains provisional.
6. **Historical booklists are not current prescription:** the 2022 Edu News list and older institutional lists identify market titles and author shorthand, not official national requirements.
