## 5. Curriculum, resource type and rights boundaries

1. These are commercial reference/textbook records, not evidence that the publisher or a particular book is officially prescribed by NCTB.
2. The official NCTB textbook and cohort syllabus remain the curriculum anchor.
3. Keep theory First Paper, theory Second Paper, practical manual, theory/numerical solution book, test paper and admission practice book as separate resource types.
4. Store cohort labels such as Exam 2028 as source observations until matched to the official cohort definition.
5. Retailer ISBN fields require format/check-digit validation. Do not silently accept a 22-digit catalogue ID as ISBN.
6. Never use a retailer product code as an ISBN.
7. Do not copy, index for redistribution, or distribute copyrighted full book text without documented permission.

## 6. Chemistry content QA priorities

- **Quantitative chemistry:** independently recompute mole, stoichiometry, concentration, gas-law, equilibrium, pH, thermochemistry and electrochemistry examples; verify significant figures and units.
- **Atomic structure and periodicity:** check quantum-number restrictions, electron configurations, periodic trends and exceptions.
- **Chemical bonding:** validate Lewis structures, molecular geometry, polarity, oxidation states and equation balancing.
- **Organic chemistry:** verify nomenclature, mechanisms, reagent conditions, reaction products and exceptions; do not accept reaction maps without independent checking.
- **Chemical equilibrium and kinetics:** verify assumptions, equilibrium expressions, units, rate laws and graph interpretations.
- **Electrochemistry:** check cell notation, electrode potentials, sign conventions, Nernst calculations and Faraday-law units.
- **Environmental / industrial chemistry:** date-stamp claims and verify against authoritative sources.
- **Practical chemistry:** validate apparatus, reagent concentrations, hazards, waste handling, observations, inference, calculations, viva and report-book requirements.
- **Answer keys:** independently solve a representative sample and a targeted set of high-risk numerical/reaction questions before adopting any book's answer key.
