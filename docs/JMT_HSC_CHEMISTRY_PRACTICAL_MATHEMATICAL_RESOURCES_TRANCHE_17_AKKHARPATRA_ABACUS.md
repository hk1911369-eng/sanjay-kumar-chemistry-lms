- Mamun Books repeats 9789843376725 for both Bengali Chemistry First Paper and Abacus Practical Chemistry.
- Mamun Books lists the Bengali Chemistry Second Paper as ISBN 9789843376763 and names Dr. Gazi Md. Ahsanul Kabir plus Dr. Md. Rabiul Islam.
- eBoighar's July 2024 English Chemistry Second Paper lists 880 pages but does not expose an ISBN in the retrieved extract.

**Conclusion:** The duplicate assignments are not a one-off transcription in a single retailer: two retailer sources repeat the Bengali First Paper/Practical Chemistry identifier collision, and PBS repeats the English First/Second Paper collision. This strongly suggests catalogue data-entry or identifier reuse problems. It still does not tell us the correct ISBN for each book. Keep all four affected identifiers in a quarantine state until the physical barcode/copyright pages are inspected.

## 7. Resource taxonomy for Chemistry

JMT must model at least these distinct categories:
- `core_theory_textbook`: Chemistry First Paper and Chemistry Second Paper.
- `practical_manual`: practical experiments, notebook, viva and safety.
- `mathematical_solution_companion`: worked solutions to textbook numerical activities.
- `exercise_workbook`: independent practice tasks and feedback.
- `test_paper_question_bank`: board and college examination questions.
- `special_preparation_supplement`: cohort-specific assessment or curriculum activity support.
- `admission_enrichment`: admission-level material beyond the HSC syllabus.

Do not use a publisher's umbrella catalogue category as the only evidence for resource type; inspect the title and content description.

## 8. Chemistry QA requirements by resource type

### Mathematical Chemistry companions
- Recalculate every sampled problem independently.
- Verify units, significant figures, logarithms, constants, formula assumptions and rounding.
- Confirm each worked solution matches the correct theory-book edition and activity numbering.
- Distinguish an algebraic shortcut from a chemically valid assumption.
- Identify methods that depend on mathematical prerequisites not taught in the target cohort.

### Practical Chemistry manuals
- Check experiment objective, apparatus, reagent concentration, hazard warnings, sequence, observation tables, calculations and conclusions.
- Verify disposal and handling instructions against current safety references.
- Check viva questions against the actual experiment and practical syllabus.
- Do not assume a printed manual demonstrates hands-on practical competence.

### Core theory textbooks
- Validate chemical equations, structures, mechanisms, nomenclature, constants and numerical answers independently.
- Map chapter content to official cohort outcomes before recommending a title.
