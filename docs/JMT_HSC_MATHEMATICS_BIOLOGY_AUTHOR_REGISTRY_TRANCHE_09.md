## 5. Identity and edition safeguards

1. "Ketab Sir" resolves to the author name Md. Ketab Uddin in multiple retailer records; SKL listings also name Professor Md. Nazrul Islam. Preserve both names and source-specific bylines.
2. "S. U. Ahmed" is associated with Alpha Publications Higher Mathematics; older Bengali and current English-version records may have different co-author/edition data.
3. Dr. Mohammad Abul Hasan's Biology First Paper and Gazi S. M. Azmol's Biology Second Paper are distinct author/title series.
4. A title labelled English Version must not be stored as English language if the retailer's language field says Bengali. Mark it disputed and inspect the copy.
5. Solution books, main textbooks, parallel texts, guides and test papers must remain distinct resource types.
6. Retailer cohort labels (e.g. HSC 2028) are discovery metadata, not official syllabus authority.

## 6. Content audit priorities

### Higher Mathematics
- Independently solve representative worked examples in algebra, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, vectors, calculus, probability and mechanics as applicable to the verified syllabus.
- Check domain restrictions, extraneous roots, sign conventions, diagrams and calculator assumptions.
- Verify solution-book correspondence against the exact main-text edition.
- Detect unsupported shortcut formulas and confirm their conditions.

### Biology
- Check taxonomy and nomenclature against current accepted terminology and the syllabus cohort.
- Verify labelled diagrams, life cycles, genetics notation, ecological examples and numerical population-genetics calculations.
- Separate simplified pedagogical models from biological reality.
- Date-stamp changing biotechnology and health-related examples.
