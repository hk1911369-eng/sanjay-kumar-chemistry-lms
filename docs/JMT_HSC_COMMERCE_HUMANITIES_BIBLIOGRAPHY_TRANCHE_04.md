## 1. Research objective

JMT's book registry must cover the full HSC ecosystem, not only science. This tranche starts the Commerce/Business Studies and Humanities map using Akkharpatra's own HSC catalogue and individual publisher detail pages, Panjeree's own HSC Test Papers catalogue, detailed bookseller metadata for selected Panjeree and Akkharpatra records, and retailer category pages only as discovery evidence where no exact title page was retrieved.

A publisher's own catalogue is evidence of what it lists for sale, not proof that NCTB or a board prescribes every listed title.

## 6. Quality and currency checks for Commerce/Humanities

- Accounting: standards edition, financial statement formats, recognition/measurement rules, depreciation, inventory valuation, adjustments, error correction, ledger balancing and numerical answer checks.
- Finance/Banking/Insurance: current Bangladesh banking regulations, payment systems, interest calculations, risk terminology, insurance principles and current examples; date-stamp all regulatory statements.
- Economics: distinguish definitions from empirical claims; cite data sources and dates; validate graphs, axes, elasticities, national income calculations and macroeconomic terminology.
- Business/Marketing: distinguish theory from contemporary examples; verify local law, market claims and case facts.
- Civics/Governance: legal and constitutional claims must be checked against current authoritative texts; distinguish normative discussion from law.
- Sociology/Social Work/History/Geography: verify terminology, dates, named people/places, map data, causal claims and changing statistical facts.
- English/Bangla: validate grammar explanations, literary attributions, spelling conventions, text editions and board-specific assessment requirements.

## 7. JMT catalogue schema additions

The book registry should include:
- resource_type: official_textbook | commercial_reference | test_paper | question_bank | practical_manual | solution_book | guide | coaching_material | digital_course
- stream: science | business_studies | humanities | common_compulsory | technical_bmt | alim | other
- subject, paper, language, medium, class_level, cohort, exam_year
- publisher, imprint, authors[], editors[], contributors[] with explicit role
- edition, printing, publication_date, isbn, pages
- publisher_source, retailer_sources[], title_page_verified, copyright_page_verified, contents_verified
- official_status with evidence source and verification date
- syllabus_mapping_status, content_review_status, rights_status
- conflicts[] storing each source's exact value rather than overwriting one value with another
