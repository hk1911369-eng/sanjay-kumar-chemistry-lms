## 8. Cross-source data-integrity findings

1. NCTB's official 2025–26 list and commercial 2026–27 grammar/practice products are different authority and resource classes.
2. Bangla First Paper core materials are NCTB's Sahityapath and Sahaypath in the official 2025–26 listing. Commercial Bangla Second Paper grammar/composition books do not replace them.
3. Do not assume the 2025–26 NCTB list is identical to the 2026–27 cohort's final official list; retrieve the 1 October 2026 notice attachment.
4. PBS marks the Nobodoot Bangla Second Paper product's language as English, an apparent metadata inconsistency requiring physical-copy verification.
5. Nobodoot Exam 2028 byline is missing. The Exam 2026 listing's Md. Dedarul Alam Murad byline must not be carried forward automatically.
6. Akkharpatra Bangla Grammar page count differs between publisher and retailer (992 vs 1,008).
7. 10 Minute School Bangla Master Book page count differs between retailers (500 vs 575).
8. Panjeree practice books are not NCTB core textbooks; the separate answer-key volume is another resource.
9. Collective editorial bylines (10 Minute School Research Team; Chowdhury & Hossain) should be represented as organizations/teams until individual contributor names are evidenced.
10. Publisher claims about “common questions,” “exclusive suggestions,” or complete preparation are marketing claims, not validated predictive accuracy.

## 9. Learning-quality and content QA priorities

### Bangla Second Paper
- Verify grammar rules, accepted variants, spelling standards, punctuation, sentence construction and composition structures against current official curriculum requirements.
- Check that model compositions teach adaptable structure and accurate information rather than encouraging memorized outdated facts.
- Verify every quoted board question's year, board and source.
- Review language accessibility and the quality of Bangla explanations.

### English Second Paper
- Independently validate grammar rules, exceptions, example sentences, transformation, narration, modifiers, connectors, prepositions, punctuation and composition formats.
- Check whether examples reflect the official question structure for the relevant cohort.
- Test answer keys for ambiguity and multiple acceptable answers.
- Ensure model essays, reports, emails and paragraphs use accurate, current facts and appropriate register.

### English First Paper practice
- Verify each passage and activity against the correct English for Today edition and lesson numbering.
- Check translations, vocabulary glosses, comprehension answers and poem summaries for fidelity.
- Do not reproduce full textbook passages in JMT without rights clearance.
