## 3. Important findings and unresolved conflicts

1. Hasan Book House Chemistry is often presented by retailers with a two-author byline (Hajari and Nag), while PBS product headlines show only Hajari on Bengali First/Second Paper and only Haradhan Nag on English First Paper. Title-page evidence is required to settle exact edition-level responsibility.
2. Hasan First Paper page counts vary: 656 (Sohopathi's 2025 record), 664 (PBS 2026 record), and 796 (a separate English 2025 retailer record). Do not combine them.
3. Hasan Second Paper has a PBS 2026 reprint at 796 pages and older 2025 retailer records. Keep edition year and language separate.
4. Hasan theory, practical and solution resources are distinct products.
5. Akkharpatra Bengali First Paper 2026 publisher detail says 928 pages; a 2025 retailer listing says 740 pages. These may be different editions, but inspect the physical copy to confirm.
6. Akkharpatra English First Paper 2026 lists three contributors at eBoighar, while PBS and Wafilife short bylines show only Mahbub Hasan Linkon. Store source-specific responsibility statements.
7. No product listing establishes NCTB prescription, content accuracy or educational superiority.

## 4. Chemistry QA priorities for Hasan and Akkharpatra editions

- Independently verify all chemical equations, oxidation states, reaction conditions, stoichiometric calculations and significant figures.
- For organic chemistry, validate nomenclature, structures, reagents, conditions, products and exceptions against authoritative chemistry references.
- Review diagrams and tables for scientific accuracy and legibility in both Bengali and English editions.
- Check whether each chapter's learning outcomes and exercises match the correct official cohort syllabus, not merely a generic “latest curriculum” claim.
- For practical manuals, verify hazards, reagent concentrations, apparatus, procedure, observations, calculations, disposal and viva content.
- Compare worked solutions against the exact edition's question numbering and wording; do not use a different edition's answer key without a reconciliation map.

## 5. Registry controls

- Keep author names as raw source values plus a separate person-identity candidate; do not merge Hajari/Hazari spellings without identity evidence.
- Store publisher spelling variants (Hasan/Hassan Book House) as aliases pending publisher confirmation.
- Record edition, printing/reprint, year, language, paper, pages, ISBN and cohort separately.
- Mark missing ISBN as unknown; do not infer from retailer product IDs.
- Keep practical manuals and solution books separate from core theory papers.
- Preserve conflicting retailer/publisher observations with source and retrieval date.
- Do not ingest or redistribute full copyrighted text without explicit rights.
