## 6. Data-integrity findings

1. Bengali and English versions are separate editions/resources; language must be stored at edition level.
2. A practical manual is not a theory textbook; its learning outcomes and assessment evidence differ.
3. The same publisher may list core books, split volumes, exercise books, solutions and practice books. Resource type must be explicit.
4. Preserve raw name spellings: Gazi S. M. Azmol / Gazi Ajmal; Nur-e-Parvin Khanom / Nur-e-Parvin Khanam; Irm Jahan / Irm Jahan; Azibor/Azibur.
5. Do not infer that a 2026 English-version author team is identical to the Bengali edition's team.
6. The Exam 2028 Lecture Second Paper byline conflict (Prof. Md. Ashraf Uddin vs Dr. Ashfaqul Muid Khandaker) must be resolved from a physical title page.
7. Retailer cohort labels and marketing descriptions do not establish NCTB prescription or quality.
8. The historical CESC list is a school/session-specific discovery source, not a current national list.

## 7. Content QA priorities

### Biology theory
- Verify taxonomy and nomenclature, diagrams, genetics notation, cell and tissue descriptions, physiology, ecology and biotechnology against current authoritative references and the target syllabus.
- Check that simplified diagrams do not create false anatomical or physiological relationships.
- Independently recompute genetics, population and ecology numericals; verify units and assumptions.
- Track time-sensitive biomedical and biotechnology statements with source and review date.

### Practical Biology
- Verify experiment aim, materials, safety, procedure, observation table, labelled drawings, inference, viva and record-book expectations.
- Distinguish simulation/illustration from actual practical competency.
- Review whether the manual matches the practical syllabus and the institution's examination requirements.
