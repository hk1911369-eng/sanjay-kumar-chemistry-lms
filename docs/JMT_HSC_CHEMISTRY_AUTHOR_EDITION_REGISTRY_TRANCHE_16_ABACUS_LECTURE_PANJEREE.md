## 4. Resource-type taxonomy for Chemistry

Use separate records for:
- `core_textbook`: theory First Paper or Second Paper.
- `practical_manual`: experiments, observations, diagrams, viva and practical record support.
- `solution_book`: worked solutions to a named textbook/paper.
- `practice_question_solution`: board/college questions, MCQ/CQ practice and model tests.
- `highlighted_edition`: an annotated/marked presentation of an existing text; identify the underlying publisher and separate any editing/annotation rights.

Do not merge a practice book or solution book into the theory textbook simply because the title contains “Chemistry First/Second Paper.”

## 5. Chemistry content QA requirements

### First Paper
- Independently verify atomic structure, periodic trends, chemical bonding, mole concept, stoichiometry, thermochemistry, chemical equilibrium, kinetics and electrochemistry against the correct cohort syllabus.
- Recompute numerical examples from first principles; check significant figures, units, assumptions and dimensional consistency.
- Review diagrams and orbital/electron configurations for conceptual correctness.
- Verify definitions and exceptions; avoid memorization shortcuts that create scientifically false rules.

### Second Paper
- Verify organic nomenclature, functional groups, reaction conditions, mechanisms where required, isomerism and reaction products.
- Recalculate quantitative analytical chemistry and electrochemistry examples.
- Check industrial chemistry, environmental chemistry and applied examples against authoritative, current references.
- Confirm that reaction schemes balance atoms/charge and state catalysts/conditions correctly.
- Distinguish HSC-level expected detail from university/admission enrichment.

### Practical manual
- Check experiment aim, apparatus, reagent concentrations, hazards, procedure, observation tables, calculations, inference and viva.
- Verify chemical safety and disposal instructions; do not rely on a decorative experiment description without a reproducible method.
- Confirm alignment with official practical outcomes and the learner's institution requirements.

## 6. Registry and rights controls

1. Store raw byline per source and edition; canonicalize a person only after identity evidence.
2. Store raw publisher identifiers separately from validated ISBN fields.
3. Record title, paper, language, edition, year, pages, cohort label, resource type and source timestamp independently.
4. Keep retailer claims of “Exam 2028” as source observations until reconciled with official cohort definitions.
5. A publicly visible listing or sample does not grant JMT permission to copy, index or redistribute a copyrighted book. Obtain explicit permission/licensing before ingesting full text or answer keys.
6. Keep editorial/annotation rights separate from rights in the underlying textbook.
