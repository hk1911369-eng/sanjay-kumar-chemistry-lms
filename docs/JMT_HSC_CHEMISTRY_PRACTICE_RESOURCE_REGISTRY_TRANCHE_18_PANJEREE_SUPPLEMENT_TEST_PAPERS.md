## 7. Data-integrity findings

1. Panjeree Supplement++ and Test Papers are practice resources, not core theory textbooks.
2. The HSC 2027 Bengali supplements, HSC 2027 combined English supplement and Exam 2028 English practice books are separate cohort/resource records.
3. The First Paper English practice book source identifier 260901121030 is 12 digits; it is not a standard ISBN-13.
4. The Second Paper English practice book identifier 2609011210044 is 13 digits; validate the check digit and physical barcode before treating it as an ISBN.
5. The Bengali supplement identifiers 2607011310126 and 2607011310130 should also be treated as source-reported identifiers pending barcode verification.
6. Do not infer individual authors from the Panjeree Editorial Board byline.
7. Do not treat publisher statements such as “common” or “sure marks” as evidence that questions will appear in the exam. They are marketing claims and require retrospective testing.
8. The HSC 2026 test-paper book must not contaminate a 2028 question corpus unless an item is separately verified as syllabus-valid and legally usable.

## 8. JMT ingestion and quality rules

- Store `resource_type=practice_reference` for Supplement++ products and `resource_type=test_paper` for Made Easy test papers.
- Store First Paper, Second Paper or combined First+Second Paper explicitly.
- Store the exam cohort separately from publication year and academic year.
- Preserve editorial-board attribution as an organization/responsibility body.
- Verify every answer independently before using it as an answer key.
- Record question provenance, board, year, paper, marks, syllabus topic and rights status.
- Do not scrape, reproduce or distribute full copyrighted practice-book content without permission.
- Treat commercial “suggestions” as forecast claims, not as ground truth or a reliable question predictor.
