## 4. Cross-publisher and data-integrity findings

1. Abacus PBS product records contain multiple cross-title ISBN collisions. This is a critical bibliographic QA issue, not a cosmetic difference.
2. ISBN checksum validity alone cannot prove a number is assigned to the correct title.
3. Retailer headline authors may omit co-authors described in the body or listed by another retailer.
4. Akkharpatra's publisher First Paper page count (928) conflicts with eBoighar (912).
5. Akkharpatra Bengali Second Paper (976 pages) and English Second Paper (928 pages) are separate language editions.
6. Lecture's long numeric product identifiers are not ISBN-13 values; store them separately.
7. Lecture core textbooks, Creative solution/practice books, practical-support books and test papers are distinct resource types.
8. Do not infer NCTB endorsement, quality or predictive accuracy from a commercial product listing or publisher marketing copy.

## 5. Chemistry content QA requirements

### Conceptual and quantitative checks
- Verify atomic structure, periodic trends, chemical bonding, states of matter, thermodynamics, equilibrium, kinetics, electrochemistry, organic nomenclature, reaction mechanisms and applied chemistry against the target syllabus.
- Recalculate every sampled stoichiometry, concentration, gas-law, equilibrium, pH, electrochemical and thermochemical numerical.
- Check significant figures, units, dimensional consistency, assumptions, standard-state conditions and rounding.
- Check oxidation numbers, electron configurations, IUPAC names, reaction conditions and balanced equations.
- For organic chemistry, verify structures, stereochemistry where relevant, reagents, products and reaction conditions; flag oversimplified universal rules.
- Independently execute or recalculate any data-driven practical/activity examples.

### Practical Chemistry
- Keep the practical manual separate from theory.
- Validate apparatus, reagent concentrations, hazards, PPE, waste disposal, procedure, observation tables, calculations, inference and viva.
- Do not import unsafe or outdated laboratory procedures into JMT.
- Distinguish simulated activities from real wet-lab competencies.

### Assessment quality
- Sample MCQs and CQs across all syllabus units.
- Independently solve each answer key; identify ambiguous stems, multiple correct options, hidden assumptions and marking-scheme mismatch.
- Check that admission-level enrichment is clearly labelled and not confused with HSC core outcomes.

## 6. Required registry model updates

For each source observation, retain:
- `edition_id`, `title_raw`, `paper`, `language`, `publisher`, `cohort_label_raw`, `edition_raw`, `publication_date_raw`, `page_count_raw`, `isbn_raw`, `source_product_identifier`, `source_url`, `source_type`, `retrieved_at`, `byline_raw`, `responsibility_statement_raw`, `verification_status`.

For normalized bibliographic records, add:
- `isbn_status`: `unverified`, `verified`, `conflicted`, `invalid_format`.
- `resource_type`: `core_textbook`, `practical_manual`, `solution_book`, `practice_reference`, `test_paper`.
- `edition_relationship`: language variant, reprint, revised edition, new edition, or unresolved.
- `conflict_group_id`: links all source observations that disagree on one bibliographic field.

Do not use ISBN as the only primary key. A stable internal edition ID must remain independent of the ISBN field.
