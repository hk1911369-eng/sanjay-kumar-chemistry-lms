## 3. Evidence and identity cautions

1. The historical CESC booklist lists the four-person Mullick Brothers Physics author group, but is specific to one institution and session (2021–22 / HSC 2023).
2. The 2019 Mullick Brothers First Paper listing is Bengali, whereas the historical institution list records an English-version option. Record language at the edition level.
3. Professor Abdul Ghani's 2015 Second Paper and 2017 First Paper are historical records. Do not treat them as current HSC 2028 texts.
4. “Sushanto Kumar Sorker” and “Sushantu Kumar Sarkar” are not yet proven to be the same author identity.
5. Haradhan Nag First Paper contributor metadata differs between retailer listings. The 2026 PBS product title identifies Prof. Haradhan Nag, while the 2025 retailer listing names Dr. Saruk Kanti Singh Hajari as co-author.
6. “Hazari Nag” in an eBoighar Second Paper record is not enough to identify a second author. Preserve the raw name and source.
7. A library catalogue is strong evidence for the specific edition it describes, but an older catalogue entry is not evidence of a current edition.
8. No publisher-origin product pages were retrieved for the Mullick Brothers titles in this tranche. Retailer claims about quality or popularity are not academic review.

## 4. Database implications

- Maintain separate canonical person records with alias candidates and unresolved identity links.
- Keep raw source spelling and normalized display name separately.
- Record contributor roles only when the source explicitly states them; do not infer co-authorship from retailer category or recommendations.
- Keep paper, language, cohort, edition, publisher imprint and product type at edition level.
- Preserve historical editions for research but prevent them from appearing as current learner recommendations unless a verified syllabus mapping supports that use.
- Record the source institution and session for school booklists.
- Add a review status for identity conflicts: `unresolved_alias`, `responsibility_statement_unverified`, `edition_unverified`, or `historical_only`.

## 5. Content-review priorities

### Physics
- Verify the sequence and mathematical treatment of mechanics, thermodynamics, electricity, magnetism, optics and modern physics against the relevant cohort syllabus.
- Independently solve sample numerical problems and check dimensions, sign conventions, vector notation and significant figures.
- Compare Bengali and English editions only after confirming that they are corresponding editions.

### Chemistry
- Audit chemical equations, reaction conditions, equilibrium, electrochemistry, organic nomenclature and mechanisms.
- Verify constants, units, state symbols, oxidation-state conventions and exceptions.
- Check whether the contents match the actual cohort's current syllabus; do not rely on retailer descriptions such as “short syllabus”.
