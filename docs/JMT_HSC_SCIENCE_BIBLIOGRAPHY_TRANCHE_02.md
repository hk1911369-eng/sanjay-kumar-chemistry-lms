## 1. Evidence policy for this tranche

- Retailer pages help discover titles, authors, editions and imprints. They do not prove NCTB approval, official prescription, quality, or exact contents.
- A book described as “textbook” by a seller may still be a commercial reference or supplementary text. Preserve the source's own classification and JMT's independently verified status separately.
- Edition/session tags matter. For example, a title marked “Exam 2028” must not automatically be assigned to HSC 2026.
- ISBN, title, author spelling/order and publisher must be checked against the physical title/copyright page before deduplication.
- The catalogue must not ingest pirated scans or reproduce full copyrighted content. Use licensed copies, publisher samples, lawful excerpts, metadata and independently authored explanations.

### ICT interpretation

ICT needs a special technical-validation track because the subject includes theory and practical computing. Validate HTML/CSS/markup examples in current browsers; C programs with compiler/version and expected output; database/SQL syntax against a stated DBMS; binary/logic calculations; network/security claims; and screenshot-based interface instructions against current software. A printed solution that works only in a deprecated environment must be tagged as version-specific. Do not present “works in JMT simulation” as proof that a student can operate real software.
