## 7. Evidence and confidence rules reinforced

| Evidence type | Use in JMT | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher-owned product page | Title and publisher's own metadata; best first-party discovery | Independent quality, NCTB prescription, exact contents if not shown |
| Publisher category catalogue | Publisher portfolio and product existence | Full author list, current edition, syllabus alignment for every title |
| Detailed bookseller listing | ISBN, edition/pages/language clues and cross-checks | Publisher authority or complete authorship |
| Institutional booklist | That a named institution used/recommended a title for a specific session | National prescription or current cohort relevance |
| Community/student blog | Shorthand names, popularity leads, learner discourse | Accuracy, quality, official status, national consensus |
| Physical title/copyright/contents pages | Strong bibliographic and contents evidence for that copy | Independent academic correctness |
| Expert-reviewed chapter sample | Evidence for content quality within the sample scope | Quality of the entire book |
