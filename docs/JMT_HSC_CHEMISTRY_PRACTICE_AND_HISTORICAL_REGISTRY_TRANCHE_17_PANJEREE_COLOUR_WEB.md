## 5. Question-bank and predictor design implications

Panjeree's product descriptions illustrate why JMT's resource registry must record what a book actually contains rather than simply calling every resource a textbook.

Suggested resource types:
- `core_textbook`
- `practice_book`
- `special_supplement`
- `test_paper_compilation`
- `solution_book`
- `practical_manual`
- `historical_textbook`

For any question bank or exam-prediction feature:
1. Record the source book's exact edition, cohort, language and page/question provenance.
2. Separate past board questions from college test questions, original practice questions and publisher suggestions.
3. Treat publisher claims such as “common questions” or “sure marks” as marketing language, not validated forecast performance.
4. Deduplicate identical questions while preserving all source/page occurrences and copyright provenance.
5. Keep answer keys and worked solutions separately versioned and independently reviewed.
6. Never use a practice-book's chapter emphasis as a substitute for the official assessment blueprint.
7. Do not redistribute copyrighted questions or book scans without permission or a documented lawful basis.

## 6. Data-integrity findings

1. Panjeree practice books and Special Supplements are not theory textbooks.
2. Cohort-specific HSC 2026/2027 resources must not be silently reused for Exam 2028.
3. Panjeree identifiers have inconsistent lengths across retailer records; validate before storing as ISBN.
4. Panjeree Editorial Board is an editorial responsibility statement, not a named individual author.
5. Colour Web's 2015 First and Second Paper library records are distinct editions with different edition numbers and extents.
6. Preserve raw author spelling variants: Swapan Kumer Mistry / Swapan Kumar Mistry.
7. Do not infer a current Second Paper from the existence of a current First Paper listing.
