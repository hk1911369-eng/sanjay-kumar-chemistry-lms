## 4. Critical data-integrity findings

1. **Abacus ISBN collisions:** 9789843376725 appears on both Bengali Chemistry First Paper and Practical Chemistry. 9789843376879 appears on both English First Paper and English Second Paper. These are source-level collisions requiring physical barcode checks.
2. Abacus bylines are inconsistent: PBS product headline often names Dr. Gazi Md. Ahsanul Kabir, while product descriptions and established retailer author catalogues name both Dr. Gazi Md. Ahsanul Kabir and Dr. Md. Rabiul Islam.
3. Hasan Book House Bengali PBS records headline Dr. Saroj Kanti Singh Hajari, while Mamun Books lists him with Professor Haradhan Nag. English eBoighar listings use “Hazari Nag”; do not assume this is a valid separate person or automatically normalize it.
4. Hasan 2025 and 2026 editions have different page counts and edition labels; preserve as separate records.
5. Lecture's 2026 raw identifier strings are 22 digits, not ISBN-13. Never store them in an ISBN field without validation.
6. A practical manual, core theory textbook, solution supplement, special-preparation supplement and test-paper book are distinct resource types.
7. Retailer claims of popularity, “sure success”, exam preparation or syllabus coverage are not independent evidence of content quality or prediction accuracy.
8. Retailer cohort labels are source observations. Official syllabus mapping remains a separate verification task.

## 5. Chemistry content QA priorities

For each paper and edition:
- Independently check atomic structure, periodic trends, chemical bonding, thermochemistry, equilibrium, electrochemistry, organic reaction mechanisms, nomenclature, industrial chemistry and environmental chemistry against the target syllabus.
- Verify every equation is balanced and reaction conditions/catalysts are stated correctly.
- Check oxidation states, units, constants, significant figures, logarithms and numerical rounding.
- Validate organic structures, stereochemical notation, reaction arrows and product predictions.
- Recompute quantitative examples independently; do not trust a printed answer key without checking.
- Check that examples do not import university-admission content as mandatory HSC material.
- For practical manuals, validate reagents, apparatus, hazards, waste disposal, observation tables, calculations and viva questions separately.

## 6. Database requirements

- Store each paper, language, edition, cohort and resource type as its own record.
- Store raw bylines and normalized person candidates separately.
- Store ISBN as a validated 10/13-character identifier only; raw retailer IDs belong in a separate `source_product_identifier` field.
- Support `identifier_conflict`, `responsibility_statement_unverified`, `edition_conflict`, `page_count_conflict`, `language_variant` and `historical_only` states.
- Preserve the URL, retrieval date, exact source wording and source authority for every bibliographic assertion.
- Do not mark any commercial book as NCTB-prescribed or officially endorsed based solely on a bookseller category.
