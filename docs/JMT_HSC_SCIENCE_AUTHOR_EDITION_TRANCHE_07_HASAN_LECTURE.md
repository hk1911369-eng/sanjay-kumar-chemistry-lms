- Hasan Physics First Paper has a full three-person author list on Rokomari; PBS page metadata does not consistently expose that full list. Store source-specific contributor fields.
- Hasan Physics First Paper Bengali page counts conflict (895 vs 852). The displayed PBS ISBN also appears malformed.
- Hasan Physics Second Paper 2026 has a valid-looking ISBN-13 and 864 pages, but verify co-authors from title page.
- Lecture Chemistry 2026 records contain strings that should not be accepted as ISBN-13 values. The 2025 second-paper record has a plausible ISBN-13 and must remain edition-specific.
- English and Bengali editions should be distinct records with their own ISBN, translators/editors, page counts and contents maps.

### Chemistry

- Check balanced equations, oxidation states, equilibrium expressions, thermochemical signs, organic nomenclature and reaction conditions.
- Verify units, significant figures and calculator rounding.
